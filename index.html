Bet your friend you’ll stay off your phone.

Pick the apps that eat your day and a daily limit. Go over and they lock until midnight. Admit it, and you owe your friend: a beer, a coffee, whatever you bet. Only they can hand you a code to get back in early.

How it works

  1. Start a bet: your limit and what the loser owes.
  2. Send your friend the code. They join free.
  3. Go over your limit and your apps lock on your phone.
  4. Admit it. The score updates and the loser buys.
