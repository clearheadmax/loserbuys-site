Loser BuysPrivacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Your screen time stays on your phone.

Loser Buys is a game between two friends: you bet on how long you spend in the apps you choose, and whoever goes over owes the other something. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Loser Buys is made by Diserve Ventures Oy (Business ID 2378021-4), Finland. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: max@diserve.com.

What never leaves your phone

Loser Buys uses Apple’s Screen Time tools to watch the apps you pick and lock them when you pass your limit. Which apps you picked, how long you use them and when you go over are handled by iOS on your device. Loser Buys never sends any of it to us or to your friend. Your friend only learns that you lost if you choose to tell them in the app.

What we store

  • An anonymous account. A random ID created when you start or join a bet. No email, phone number or password.
  • The name you give. Shown to the friend you’re betting with.
  • Your time zone. So a loss is dated on your own calendar.
  • The bet. Its code, daily limit and stake (for example “a beer”), the days each of you admitted losing, whether a loss was paid, requests for an unlock code, the codes your friend handed out, and when you last poked your friend.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when your friend joins, loses, asks for a code or pokes you.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have Loser Buys Pro and until when. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID.

We use this only to run the game. We don’t sell it, don’t show ads, and don’t use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who helps us

  • Supabase hosts our database and server, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Expo delivers push notifications to Apple’s notification service.
  • RevenueCat checks your App Store subscription for us. It receives your anonymous account ID and your purchase history from Apple.
  • Apple processes subscriptions and Screen Time on your device under its own privacy policy.

Expo and RevenueCat are based in the United States. Transfers there are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis

We process this data to provide the game you asked to play (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Push notifications are sent only if you allow them on your phone.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Tap Delete my data in the app: your account, name and push token are removed at once, along with the bets, losses and codes tied to you, and any bet you’re in ends for your friend too. Subscription records kept by Apple and RevenueCat follow their own rules.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to max@diserve.com. Since your account is anonymous, tell us your name in the app and your friend’s, and your bet code if you still have it. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman at tietosuoja.fi.

Children

Loser Buys is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Money

No money moves through Loser Buys. The stake is just words you agree on; you settle up between yourselves. The only payment in the app is the optional Pro subscription, sold by Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
